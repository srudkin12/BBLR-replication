# Archive policy

## GitHub repository

Keep:
- readable canonical source code;
- frozen configuration/selection files required to understand or rerun analyses;
- compact manuscript-facing evidence;
- table/figure provenance maps;
- environment/reproduction instructions;
- exact archive hashes and provenance.

Do not duplicate:
- full large production HANDbacks;
- replication-level shards that are not required for ordinary reproduction;
- checkpoints;
- virtual environments;
- superseded packages;
- Paper 2 or Paper 3 computational material.

## Long-term archival tier

Full accepted HANDbacks should remain preserved outside GitHub. A later Zenodo, OSF or institutional deposit can hold selected full archives if the authors want byte-complete preservation associated with the publication.

`provenance/EXTERNAL_ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.csv` is the bridge between the compact GitHub repository and those full local/archival objects.
