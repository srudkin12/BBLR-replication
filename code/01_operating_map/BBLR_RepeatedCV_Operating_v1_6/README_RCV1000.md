# BBLR Repeated-CV Operating Characteristics v1.6

This stage moves from the 12 landmark pilot to a 92-cell, 31-DGP operating panel with 1,000 Monte Carlo replications per cell. Repeated 10x5 CV remains the primary feasible BBLR tuning rule.

## Eight blocks

A. Smooth shape controls: polynomial curvature, frequency, amplitude, transitions and local features.
B. Non-smooth stress tests: kinks and level discontinuities.
C. Density geometry: normal, skewed and clustered/gapped X for representative functions.
D. Central support ladder: soft depletion through hard disconnection.
E. Transition width x support: broad through very sharp marginal-effect changes.
F. Transition location x gap: transition relative to gap interior/edge/outside.
G. Noise scaling: sigma=0.10 and 0.50 around representative regimes.
H. Sample scaling: n=250, 1000 and 2000 around representative regimes.

The panel includes regimes expected to favour BBLR and regimes deliberately expected to expose its weaknesses.
